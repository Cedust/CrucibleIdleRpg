---
name: grill-me
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea. Use when the user wants to stress-test their thinking or uses any "grill" trigger phrase.
---

Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect until reaching a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree and resolve dependencies between decisions one by one. Provide a recommended answer with every question.

Ask exactly one question at a time and wait for the answer before continuing.

Look up facts in the available environment instead of asking the user. Put decisions to the user and wait for their answer.

Do not act on the plan, decision, or idea until the user confirms that shared understanding has been reached.
